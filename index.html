I am an economist currently based at the Institute of Real Estate and Urban Studies, National University of Singapore. Prior to joining NUS, I was an Associate Professor of Economics at Fudan University. I received my PhD in Economics from Boston University.

My research focuses on understudied markets and populations, bringing evidence from these settings to broader questions about economic behavior and institutions. I study how incentives, social norms, discrimination, and institutional arrangements shape individual behavior and market outcomes.

Much of my research grows out of sustained field engagement with the communities and institutions I study. This work often involves developing original data and field experiments through long-term partnerships with government agencies and community organizations. More recently, my research has expanded to social mobility and social networks, including the development of a long-run panel study on the determinants of social mobility.
